How it works

Four steps to a brand-new look

Install any icon pack from Google Play as usual, then open Magic Themer.

Real screenshots from an HONOR Magic V5 (with Arcticons) and the Android emulator's built-in test pack. Tap any screen to zoom.

  1. 1

    Choose a pack

    Tap Choose pack and pick any installed icon pack. Got a theme already? Open .hnt lets you edit its icons, name, designer and description, keeping its wallpapers, fonts and other modules.

  2. 2

    Fill the gaps

    Needs icon lists apps the pack doesn't cover. Tap one to get suggestions based on its name, its category and whether it plays music or video, or search the whole pack, alternatives included.

    Follow the glowing ring and tap through it yourself →

  3. 3

    Name your theme

    In Theme, check the name and designer. New themes are named after the pack until you type your own. Keep Whole icon pack on to include apps you haven't installed yet, or turn it off to export only what's on your phone.

  4. 4

    Save & apply

    Tap Save theme and save into internal storage → HONOR/Themes (or Downloads, then move it there with Files). Magic Themer reads the file back and checks its size and SHA-256 before it says it's saved.

    Then open HONOR Themes → Me → My downloads, go to Customize → Icons and pick your theme. Done!

    Theme not showing up? Tap App info in the save dialog, choose Force stop, then reopen Themes.

Features

Small app, lots of tricks

  • Finds your packs

    Reads installed packs in the common launcher formats: Nova, ADW, Lawnchair, Smart Launcher and others.

  • Spots the gaps

    Needs icon shows uncovered apps with suggestions that make sense for each one.

  • Original icons

    See each app's own icon instead of your current theme's, so you know what a theme really covers.

  • Forgiving search

    Finds apps and artwork even with typos, split words or initials.

  • Edits existing themes

    Opens ordinary .hnt files and always saves a new copy. The original is never overwritten.

  • Honest about limits

    Flags apps HONOR can't theme, like builds renamed after compilation.

  • Light & dark

    Follows your phone's theme, day or night.

  • Foldable friendly

    Adapts to foldables, tablets and landscape, with more columns when there's room.

Good to know

The honest bits

Which phones does it work on?

It's tested on an HONOR Magic V5 with MagicOS 10 (EU). Other HONOR and Huawei devices may work but haven't been tested. It needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Does it unlock paid icon packs?

No. Install packs from Google Play as usual; Magic Themer only reads what's already on your phone. Icon artwork belongs to its creators, so keep themes made from packs for personal use unless the pack's author allows sharing.

What doesn't carry over from a pack?

Icons are static 192 px PNGs. Dynamic calendars and clocks, pack masks, backgrounds and scaling for unmapped apps aren't reproduced. HONOR themes one icon per app package, so apps with several launcher activities share one icon.

Can it make wallpapers, fonts or lock screens?

No. New wallpapers, fonts, lock screens and system UI can't be created. When you edit an existing theme, its wallpapers, fonts and other modules are kept as they are. Encrypted, signed or otherwise unusual theme formats aren't supported.

I used v0.7.0 or earlier. What changes?

From v0.7.1 the app ID is com.tzii.magicthemer, so Android installs it next to the old dev.magicthemer app. Export any themes you want to keep from the old app and open them in the new one. Settings and assignments don't transfer automatically.
